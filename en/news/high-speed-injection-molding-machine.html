High Speed Injection Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the high speed injection molding machine decision for a injection molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a high speed injection molding machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 1403–2053 units per hour for 16 oz product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a high speed injection molding machine, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 3.0 ml at 1403 units per hour, not only at 2053.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The high speed injection molding machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the injection molding shops workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a high speed injection molding machine is downtime avoided during a 20-minute format change on a 16 oz format, because the injection molding shops production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

The high speed injection molding machine was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a injection molding shops operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 1403 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this high speed injection molding machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 3.0 ml on 16 oz product at 1403–2053 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Does the high speed injection molding machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

What spare parts should be held for the high speed injection molding machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1403 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
