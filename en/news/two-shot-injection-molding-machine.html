Two Shot Injection Molding Machine

Published 2026-11-20 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the two shot injection molding machine decision for a injection molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

A injection molding shops production line gains nothing from a two shot injection molding machine that performs well on paper. This build is specified at 5720–7220 units per hour for 500 ml can product with the deviation defined, so the performance can be measured rather than assumed.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a two shot injection molding machine on the specification that limits your output: the 1.5 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact parts and the 10-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a two shot injection molding machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the injection molding shops production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a two shot injection molding machine is arithmetic: every unit of 1.5 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 10-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the injection molding shops production line.

Maintenance and Service

A two shot injection molding machine that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action
Weld lines visible on the partFlow front splitting around the coreMove the gate to the weld line or raise the injection speed within the machine limit
Short shot on thin wallsMelt temperature too high or hold pressure lowReduce the melt temperature by 5–8 °C and check the hold pressure profile against the gate
Nozzle drool during idleResidual pressure in the screwRun the decompression cycle and verify the back-pressure setting
Mould opens before the part is ejectedEjector stroke too early or the sensor mis-setAdjust the ejector delay and re-trigger the safety gate sensor

Delivery and Acceptance Checklist

  • Verify the control parameter backup on the 4-inch HMI can be exported and restored
  • Confirm the 316L stainless steel contact parts are accompanied by material certificates
  • Confirm the two shot injection molding machine holds the shot weight across the full 5720–7220 range with production resin

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 5720–7220 kN.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
