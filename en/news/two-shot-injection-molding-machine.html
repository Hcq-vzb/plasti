Two Shot Injection Molding Machine

Published 2026-11-20 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the two shot injection molding machine decision for a injection molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

A two shot injection molding machine earns a place on the injection molding shops production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 5720–7220 units per hour on 500 ml can product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

A two shot injection molding machine is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±1.5 ml, 5720–7220 units per hour, 16–23 kW, and 1200 × 2800 × 2800 mm, on 500 ml can product.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a two shot injection molding machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the injection molding shops production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the two shot injection molding machine on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 500 ml can product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this two shot injection molding machine the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 10-minute routine service realistic on a 5720 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the two shot injection molding machine be upgraded later?

Within the 5720–7220 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 500 ml can range before ordering.

How does the two shot injection molding machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the two shot injection molding machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How long does a format changeover take on this two shot injection molding machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 500 ml can format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
