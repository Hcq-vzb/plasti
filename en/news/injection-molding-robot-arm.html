Injection Molding Robot Arm

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machinery

Choosing the right injection molding robot arm is a practical decision for injection molding shops. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting an injection molding robot arm on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

An injection molding robot arm should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 60–120 mm diameter product at 3020–4520 units per hour, which is the span most injection molding shops production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

An injection molding robot arm is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 3020–4520 units per hour, contact parts 304 stainless steel, control 4-inch HMI, load 5.5–7.5 kW, footprint 3200 × 1900 × 3200 mm, changeover 10 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 3200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after an injection molding robot arm arrives on a injection molding shops floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the injection molding robot arm on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 60–120 mm diameter product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for an injection molding robot arm because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 4-inch HMI that records the last 10 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the injection molding robot arm?

The reference build draws 5.5–7.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Is the injection molding robot arm suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 3020–4520 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

What is the delivery lead time for the injection molding robot arm?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 60–120 mm diameter set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
