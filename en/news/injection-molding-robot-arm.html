Injection Molding Robot Arm

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machinery

Choosing the right injection molding robot arm is a practical decision for injection molding shops. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting an injection molding robot arm on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

Position an injection molding robot arm by what it removes from the injection molding shops process. This configuration handles 60–120 mm diameter product at 3020–4520 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. An injection molding robot arm with a 10-minute changeover across 60–120 mm diameter format and 304 stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 3020–4520 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

An injection molding robot arm that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width, which is what lets the injection molding shops production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. An injection molding robot arm at 3020–4520 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 60–120 mm diameter product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the injection molding shops operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Availability planning for an injection molding robot arm starts with the assumption that a seal will need replacing in the next quarter. Holding the 304 stainless steel contact-part spares on site is what keeps a 10-minute repair a 10-minute repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 3020–4520 kN.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
