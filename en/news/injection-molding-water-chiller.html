Injection Molding Water Chiller

Published 2026-11-14 · KIWL Machinery

Two injection molding water chiller quotations can quote the same rate and still describe different machines. This guide covers the injection molding shops details that tell them apart — accuracy, materials, control and changeover — and what to write into the order.

What This Equipment Does

An injection molding water chiller earns its place by removing a manual handling step. This build handles 500 ml can product at 3971–5471 units per hour, with a 20-minute changeover between formats.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This injection molding water chiller draws 22–33 kW across a 1600 × 3600 × 2400 mm footprint with 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This injection molding water chiller already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for an injection molding water chiller is the downtime it prevents during a 20-minute changeover, because at 3971 units per hour the injection molding shops production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

The injection molding water chiller keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 3971 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the injection molding water chiller need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 3600 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

Is the injection molding water chiller suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 3971–5471 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the injection molding water chiller run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The injection molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
