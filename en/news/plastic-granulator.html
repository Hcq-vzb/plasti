Plastic Granulator

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

A plastic granulator for injection molding shops is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

A plastic granulator earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1292–1942 units per hour for 16 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

For a plastic granulator aimed at injection molding shops operations, the practical reference build is 316L stainless steel contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 1.5–2.5 kW connected load inside 2400 × 1900 × 2100 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Signal compatibility decides whether an auxiliary unit is a genuine part of the injection molding shops production line or an appliance standing next to it.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 12-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the injection molding shops production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a plastic granulator is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 1292–1942 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the rated capacity of this unit?

The reference build handles 1292–1942 units per hour depending on the configuration.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can it be integrated with the line PLC?

Yes, standard signal interfaces allow it to run under the line PLC.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
