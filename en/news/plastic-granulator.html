Plastic Granulator

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

There is no universal plastic granulator for injection molding shops. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

Position a plastic granulator by what it removes from the injection molding shops process. This configuration handles 16 oz product at 1292–1942 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

The reference specification for this plastic granulator is 1292–1942 units per hour, 16 oz format, 316L stainless steel parts, a 4-inch HMI control and a 2400 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the plastic granulator determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 1292–1942 units per hour and a 12-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A plastic granulator with a 4-inch HMI and a 12-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Keep the filter set and the seals on site for the plastic granulator, so a fault is a 12-minute repair rather than a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the plastic granulator?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Can the plastic granulator be upgraded later?

Within the 1292–1942 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 16 oz range before ordering.

Is the plastic granulator suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1292–1942 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
