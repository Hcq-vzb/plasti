What This Equipment Does

Key Technical Specifications

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action
Nozzle drool during idleResidual pressure in the screwRun the decompression cycle and verify the back-pressure setting
Mould opens before the part is ejectedEjector stroke too early or the sensor mis-setAdjust the ejector delay and re-trigger the safety gate sensor
Part weight inconsistent between cavitiesCavities not filling symmetricallyBalance the runner and re-check the nozzle seat temperature on each cavity
Cycle time drifting longerHydraulic oil heating up over a long runCheck the oil cooler and the hydraulic filter, and log the cycle time per shot

Frequently Asked Questions

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 4123–5623 kN.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
