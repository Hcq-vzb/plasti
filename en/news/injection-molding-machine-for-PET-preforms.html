Injection Molding Machine for PET Preforms

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

Where an injection molding machine for PET preforms specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the injection molding shops requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

An injection molding machine for PET preforms occupies one defined station in the production line for injection molding shops. This build runs at 3098–4598 units per hour for 250–500 ml product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This injection molding machine for PET preforms draws 15–20 kW across a 1200 × 2800 × 3200 mm footprint with 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

An injection molding machine for PET preforms that shares its control protocol with the rest of the injection molding shops production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 20-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the injection molding shops production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the injection molding machine for PET preforms leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the injection molding machine for PET preforms?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 250–500 ml set is included.

Can the injection molding machine for PET preforms run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The injection molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 250–500 ml product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
