Plastic Crusher Granulator

Published 2026-11-28 · KIWL Machinery

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the plastic crusher granulator decision for a injection molding shops buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

A plastic crusher granulator occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This injection molding shops build runs at 6554–9554 units per hour on 12 oz product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

A plastic crusher granulator is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±0.5 ml, 6554–9554 units per hour, 9.5–12.5 kW, and 3200 × 2800 × 3200 mm, on 12 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the plastic crusher granulator does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing injection molding shops conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The injection molding shops operation should count the hours the plastic crusher granulator is unavailable, not the hours it runs. At 6554–9554 units per hour the exposure is large.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the plastic crusher granulator leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.
