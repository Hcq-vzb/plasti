Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this injection molding cycle time optimization?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 2 L format takes longer.

What spare parts should be held for the injection molding cycle time optimization?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 15930 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How does the injection molding cycle time optimization behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the injection molding cycle time optimization determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
