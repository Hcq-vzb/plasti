Hot Runner System

Published 2026-11-24 · KIWL Machinery

The difference between a workable hot runner system and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for injection molding shops, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

A hot runner system earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1515–2165 units per hour for 330 ml can product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a hot runner system on the specification that limits your output: the 0.5 ml tolerance, the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This hot runner system takes a standard encoder signal and a 1200 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A hot runner system rated at 1515–2165 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this hot runner system are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 1515–2165 kN.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
