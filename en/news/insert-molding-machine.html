Insert Molding Machine

Published 2026-11-21 · KIWL Machinery

Three questions decide most insert molding machine purchases, and they are the same for every injection molding shops operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A injection molding shops production line gains nothing from an insert molding machine that performs well on paper. This build is specified at 12518–16518 units per hour for 60–120 mm diameter product with the deviation defined, so the performance can be measured rather than assumed.

Key Technical Specifications

Price an insert molding machine on the specification that limits your output: the 0.3 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime an insert molding machine can absorb. With 12518–16518 units per hour and a 15-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. An insert molding machine with a 4-inch HMI and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a injection molding shops buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the insert molding machine concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the insert molding machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 60–120 mm diameter set is included.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 60–120 mm diameter product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What does the insert molding machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
