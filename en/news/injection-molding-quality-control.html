Injection Molding Quality Control

Published 2026-11-17 · KIWL Machinery

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same injection molding shops figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

The value of an injection molding quality control is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 18100–22600 units per hour on 330 ml product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

An injection molding quality control is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 18100–22600 units per hour, contact parts 304/316L stainless steel, control 7-inch colour touch, load 6.5–8.5 kW, footprint 1200 × 2200 × 2400 mm, changeover 12 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the injection molding quality control can hand over at 18100–22600 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare an injection molding quality control against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 18100 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a injection molding shops operation.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the injection molding quality control concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action
Nozzle drool during idleResidual pressure in the screwRun the decompression cycle and verify the back-pressure setting
Short shot on thin wallsMelt temperature too high or hold pressure lowReduce the melt temperature by 5–8 °C and check the hold pressure profile against the gate
Weld lines visible on the partFlow front splitting around the coreMove the gate to the weld line or raise the injection speed within the machine limit
Cycle time drifting longerHydraulic oil heating up over a long runCheck the oil cooler and the hydraulic filter, and log the cycle time per shot

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 18100–22600 kN.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
