Injection Molding Mold Base

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machinery

An injection molding mold base for injection molding shops is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what an injection molding mold base removes from the injection molding shops process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 500 ml can product at 5223–6723 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

For a injection molding shops buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: SUS304 with sanitary fittings product-contact parts, 10-inch colour touch controls, 3.0 ml tolerance, 12-minute changeover, 5.5–7.5 kW connected load and a 1800 × 2200 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the injection molding mold base determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 5223–6723 units per hour and a 12-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. An injection molding mold base with a 10-inch colour touch and a 12-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a injection molding shops buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this injection molding mold base the wear items are simple to identify — seals, drive, guide rails — and most injection molding shops downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action

Frequently Asked Questions

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 5223–6723 kN.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
