Injection Molding Mold Base

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machinery

An injection molding mold base for injection molding shops is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

What an injection molding mold base does to the injection molding shops production line is measurable at the packing end. At 5223–6723 units per hour for 500 ml can product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This injection molding mold base draws 5.5–7.5 kW across a 1800 × 2200 × 2800 mm footprint with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

An injection molding mold base becomes part of the injection molding shops production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. An injection molding mold base with a 10-inch colour touch and a 12-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Put the injection molding mold base on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 5223–6723 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the injection molding mold base do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Can the injection molding mold base run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The injection molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

What accuracy can this injection molding mold base hold in real production?

The reference build holds 3.0 ml on 500 ml can product at 5223–6723 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
