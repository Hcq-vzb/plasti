Injection Molding Hopper Dryer

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machinery

The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For an injection molding hopper dryer in injection molding shops production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

Because an injection molding hopper dryer handles a defined step for injection molding shops, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 1 L product at 1833–2483 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this injection molding hopper dryer the tolerance is 1.0 ml, the contact parts are SUS304 with sanitary fittings, and the controls run on a 7-inch colour touch.

Integration With Your Line

Shared CIP and control logic are worth agreeing early. An injection molding hopper dryer that speaks the same protocol as the rest of the injection molding shops production line can be called up in a recipe change instead of being restarted by hand.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for an injection molding hopper dryer is the downtime it prevents during a 8-minute changeover. At 1833 units per hour, the injection molding shops production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

For injection molding shops operations the practical measure of after-sales support is how quickly an injection molding hopper dryer returns to 1833 units per hour after a fault — which depends on part availability, not on the number of people in the service team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Can the injection molding hopper dryer run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The injection molding shops team still owns the routine checks, but the 8-minute changeover is guided.

Does the injection molding hopper dryer need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
