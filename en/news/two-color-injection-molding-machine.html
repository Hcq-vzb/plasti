Two Color Injection Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machinery

There is no universal two color injection molding machine for injection molding shops. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a two color injection molding machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 4449 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the injection molding shops production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A two color injection molding machine with a 8-minute changeover across 250–500 ml format and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 4449–5949 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a two color injection molding machine can absorb. With 4449–5949 units per hour and a 8-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A two color injection molding machine handling 4449–5949 units per hour with a 1.0 ml tolerance attacks all three for a injection molding shops operation.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive maintenance on a two color injection molding machine is mostly inspection: nozzle condition, check ring wear, and the calibration record for the 10-inch colour touch that drives injection timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 4449–5949 kN.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
