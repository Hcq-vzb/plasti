Injection Molding Screw And Barrel

Published 2026-11-01 · KIWL Machinery

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same injection molding shops figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

An injection molding screw and barrel turns polymer into a finished moulded part. The clamp unit holds the tool shut against the injection force, the screw doses and injects, and the part is released before the cycle repeats.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare an injection molding screw and barrel on rated capacity alone. A 15049–19049 units per hour machine with a 1.5 ml tolerance and 304 stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating an injection molding screw and barrel as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1200 mm working width, it becomes part of the injection molding shops control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for an injection molding screw and barrel is arithmetic: every unit of 1.5 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 12-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the injection molding shops production line.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because an injection molding screw and barrel with 304 stainless steel contact parts and 15049–19049 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action
Weld lines visible on the partFlow front splitting around the coreMove the gate to the weld line or raise the injection speed within the machine limit
Short shot on thin wallsMelt temperature too high or hold pressure lowReduce the melt temperature by 5–8 °C and check the hold pressure profile against the gate
Nozzle drool during idleResidual pressure in the screwRun the decompression cycle and verify the back-pressure setting

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 15049–19049 kN.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
