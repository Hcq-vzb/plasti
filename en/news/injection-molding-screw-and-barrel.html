Injection Molding Screw And Barrel

Published 2026-11-01 · KIWL Machinery

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same injection molding shops figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

For a injection molding shops operation, the injection molding screw and barrel is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 15049–19049 units per hour on 60–120 mm diameter product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference injection molding screw and barrel uses 304 stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 5.5–7.5 kW connected load and a 1200 × 2200 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the injection molding screw and barrel does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1200 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing injection molding shops conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the injection molding screw and barrel against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 12 minutes per format change and 15049 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the injection molding screw and barrel concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the injection molding screw and barrel need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 2200 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

What spare parts should be held for the injection molding screw and barrel?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 15049 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the injection molding screw and barrel handle a 60–120 mm diameter product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
