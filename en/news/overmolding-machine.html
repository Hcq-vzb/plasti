Overmolding Machine

Published 2026-11-22 · KIWL Machinery

Specifying a overmolding machine for injection molding shops is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The unit an overmolding machine performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 500 ml can product and delivers 14524–18524 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 14524–18524 units per hour means little without 500 ml can product, a 0.3 ml tolerance and a 12-minute changeover — all three stated for this overmolding machine.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 0.3 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 14524–18524 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 12-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the injection molding shops production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this overmolding machine are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this overmolding machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 500 ml can product at 14524–18524 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the overmolding machine be upgraded later?

Within the 14524–18524 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 500 ml can range before ordering.

What does the overmolding machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
