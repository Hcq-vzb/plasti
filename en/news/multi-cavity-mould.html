Multi-Cavity Mould

Published 2026-10-23 · KIWL Machinery

Most disagreements about a multi-cavity mould come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the injection molding shops requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 1345–1995 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this multi-cavity mould it is: it processes 60–120 mm diameter product at 1345–1995 units per hour, consistently, without the injection molding shops team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a multi-cavity mould on rated capacity alone. A 1345–1995 units per hour machine with a 1.5 ml tolerance and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 3200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a multi-cavity mould arrives on a injection molding shops floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A multi-cavity mould with a 10-inch colour touch and a 20-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a injection molding shops buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this multi-cavity mould the wear items are simple to identify — seals, drive, guide rails — and most injection molding shops downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

  • Confirm the spare parts list for the multi-cavity mould with quoted lead times

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the multi-cavity mould handle a 60–120 mm diameter product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What is the delivery lead time for the multi-cavity mould?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 60–120 mm diameter set is included.

Does the multi-cavity mould need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
