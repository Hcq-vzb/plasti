Injection Molding Clamping Unit

Published 2026-10-31 · KIWL Machinery

An injection molding clamping unit for injection molding shops is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

An injection molding clamping unit should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 8 oz product at 10740–14240 units per hour, which is the span most injection molding shops production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for an injection molding clamping unit contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 1.5 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact-part grade, the 1.5–2.5 kW load and the 2400 × 2800 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime an injection molding clamping unit can absorb. With 10740–14240 units per hour and a 12-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the injection molding clamping unit on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 8 oz product with a 12-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on an injection molding clamping unit is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 10740–14240 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Troubleshooting: Common Faults and How to Resolve Them

SymptomLikely causeCorrective action
Mould opens before the part is ejectedEjector stroke too early or the sensor mis-setAdjust the ejector delay and re-trigger the safety gate sensor
Nozzle drool during idleResidual pressure in the screwRun the decompression cycle and verify the back-pressure setting
Short shot on thin wallsMelt temperature too high or hold pressure lowReduce the melt temperature by 5–8 °C and check the hold pressure profile against the gate
Weld lines visible on the partFlow front splitting around the coreMove the gate to the weld line or raise the injection speed within the machine limit

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 10740–14240 kN.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
