PET Preform Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

There is no universal PET preform molding machine for injection molding shops. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

Because a PET preform molding machine handles a defined step for injection molding shops, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 60–120 mm diameter product at 2643–3643 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a PET preform molding machine on the specification that limits your output: the 1.0 ml tolerance, the 304/316L stainless steel contact parts and the 10-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the PET preform molding machine can hand over at 2643–3643 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 10-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the injection molding shops production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this PET preform molding machine are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the PET preform molding machine be upgraded later?

Within the 2643–3643 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 60–120 mm diameter range before ordering.

How does the PET preform molding machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the PET preform molding machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How long does a format changeover take on this PET preform molding machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 60–120 mm diameter format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
