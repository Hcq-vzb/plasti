PET Preform Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machinery

There is no universal PET preform molding machine for injection molding shops. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a PET preform molding machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 2643 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the injection molding shops production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two PET preform molding machine quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies 304/316L stainless steel surfaces, a 7-inch colour touch, a 1.0 ml tolerance and a 10-minute changeover across 60–120 mm diameter format.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This PET preform molding machine takes a standard encoder signal and a 1200 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A PET preform molding machine with a 7-inch colour touch and a 10-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Wear items on a PET preform molding machine are known and finite. Planning the injection molding shops operation around lead times for nozzle, check ring and heater means the line is not waiting a week for a part while the shift runs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 2643–3643 kN.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
