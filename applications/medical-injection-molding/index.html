Medical Injection Molding

Medical molding is decided by documentation as much as by hardware. The machine must hold a validated process, run cleanly, and record what happened — which is why all-electric machines dominate this segment.

Why all-electric dominates medical molding

No hydraulic oil in the injection unit area means no oil-contamination risk near the product; servo-electric drives give reproducible shot profiles that a validation protocol can lock; and energy use and noise drop in cleanroom environments.

Materials

Medical grades of PP, PE and PC are molded with the same physics as commodity grades but with lot traceability and stricter change-control. Silicones and elastomers for medical use usually run on dedicated tooling with dedicated barrels.

Frequently asked questions

Why are all-electric machines preferred for medical parts?

No hydraulic oil near the product area removes a contamination source, and servo-electric drives hold reproducible shot profiles that a validation protocol can lock and audit.

Does medical molding always need a cleanroom?

It depends on the product class. Many components are molded in controlled environments rather than full cleanrooms; the requirement follows the customer's device classification, not the machine.

What records must a medical molding process keep?

Per-shot process parameters, raw-material lot traceability, and maintenance records that show the machine remained in its validated state.
